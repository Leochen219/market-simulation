## Data interpretation and evidence

Q is endogenous human procurement; predicting trading conditional on actual p,w,Q,D excludes procurement fidelity. Histories include state/actions without realized feedback. Prior orders are not fills, source slots are not verified input chronology, shared-market observations are dependent, and no result identifies a causal effect of memory on real people.
