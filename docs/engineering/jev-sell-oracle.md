# SELL oracle operations

This study predicts a single SELL component price conditioned on its exact human quantity, not a deployable quantity forecast. Prior ten rounds and verified historical profits are shown. The human quantity deliberately supplies target information; other target orders, target prices and target profit remain absent. Duplicate quantities within an event share one query and retain separate seeded component samples and scoring weights.

Use the CLI `preview`, then freeze only after independent review. One authorized `run` follows successful validation and credential/ledger prerequisites. The exclusive marker prevents reruns. The 106 physical-request cap reserves 65,536 input tokens at 42 nanodollars per token with a 300,000,000-nanodollar hard ceiling. Every complete body must fit 12,000 bytes; no truncation or retries are permitted. Partial technical failures must remain incomplete and preserve evidence. Use `replay` to validate evidence before `report`.

The 35 participants are previously exposed development data, not a fresh holdout or evidence of independent markets. Multi-order components are conditioned on each human quantity while other current orders remain undisclosed. Teacher-forced later histories may contain actual earlier target rounds.
